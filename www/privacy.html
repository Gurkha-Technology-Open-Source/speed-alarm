Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Speed Alarm is a traffic awareness app. Your location and trip data are processed entirely on your device. We do not transmit GPS data or trip history to any server.

Stored locally

  • Settings (speed limit, units, alarm preferences)
  • Trip history (distance, duration, speeds, safety scores)

You can delete trip history at any time from the Trips screen.
