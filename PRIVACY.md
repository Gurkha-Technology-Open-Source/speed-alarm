# Privacy Policy — Speed Alarm

**Last updated:** August 2026

Speed Alarm is a traffic awareness app published by Gurkha Technology Open Source.

## What we collect

Speed Alarm uses your device's **GPS location** to calculate your current speed, distance traveled, and trip statistics. This data is processed **entirely on your device**.

We do **not** transmit your location, trip history, or personal information to any server. There is no user account and no analytics SDK in this app.

## What is stored locally

The app stores the following on your device only:

- Your settings (speed limit, unit preference, alarm toggles)
- Trip history (distance, duration, average/max speed, overspeed statistics, safety score)

You can delete all trip history at any time from the Trips screen.

## Permissions

| Permission | Why |
|------------|-----|
| Location (fine) | To measure your speed |
| Notifications | Foreground-service notification while monitoring, and overspeed alerts |
| Vibration | Overspeed haptic alerts |
| Battery optimization exemption (optional) | Keeps monitoring reliable in the background on some phones |

## Data sharing

We do not sell, share, or transfer your data to third parties.
